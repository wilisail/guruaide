# Teacher intake and Zoom validation

## First participants

Recruit 5–8 current public SD/SMP teachers. Seek people with a recent performance-document task and people with a current/recent allowance issue; the same person may have both. Include ASN and non-ASN benefit experiences when possible. Invite one school operator and one principal/reviewer to check responsibility and acceptance assumptions.

This is a small convenience pilot. It cannot establish national adoption or error prevalence.

## Minimal interest form

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Preferred name and contact channel | Arrange an interview |
| School level, ownership, region and current role | Confirm pilot relevance |
| Employment status and reported allowance route, including unknown | Select the right workflow |
| Main task: performance document, allowance issue, or both | Route the session |
| Most recent task and approximate time spent | Establish a concrete example |
| Can share a redacted example? | Prepare a useful interview |
| Available time and permission to contact | Arrange the Zoom session |
| Optional recording consent | Separate from participation consent |

Do not request account passwords, OTPs, identity-card scans, full NIK/NIP, or bank statements in a public recruitment form.

## Confirming a participant is a current teacher

Use a school/teacher institutional email or consenting school-staff confirmation where available, together with a discussion of the current teaching role. NPSN identifies a school; it does not prove that a person works there. Do not turn verification into a requirement to upload sensitive identity documents. Public access to the open project remains separate from admission to a verified research cohort.

## 30-minute Zoom guide

| Minutes | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Confirm role, task, consent and what may be noted |
| 3–10 | Ask the teacher to walk through the latest real task/error, using a redacted example |
| 10–20 | Try the corresponding worksheet or working prototype; observe where help is needed |
| 20–26 | Compare the output with the actual destination and discuss corrections/recipient |
| 26–30 | Identify missing requirements, next action and a voluntary follow-up |

Record notes by default; record audio/video only with explicit consent. Avoid capturing account credentials or confidential student/financial information on screen.

## Questions that validate usefulness

- What exactly triggered this task, and what happens if it remains unfinished?
- Which parts could you complete yourself, and which required another person's action?
- Show the last completed example or exact error rather than describing a typical one.
- Would this draft/request be usable now? What must change before you would send it?
- Does preparing our inputs take less effort than your existing method?
- If the output were ready today, would you take the next official step?
- Who should review our understanding of the rules or recipient?

## Evidence to retain

Store participant code, workflow, approximate task timing, edits needed, whether the recipient/fields were correct, and what happened next. Keep the original private artifact with the participant unless separate permission and a necessary retention plan exist. Publish aggregate/anonymized results with consent.

Required operational items for real sessions: an interest-form link, a contact/booking route, a Zoom host/link, willing participants, and the interviewer's availability. These links and participants have not been created or recruited by this pack.
