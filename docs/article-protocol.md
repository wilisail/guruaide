# Public article and study protocol

Working title: **When incentives drive adoption: an open assistant for teacher performance and allowance administration in Indonesia**

Status: concept and proposed protocol. No results or teacher endorsements are claimed.

## Research question

Will teachers engage more readily with a tool for an immediate employment/allowance task than with general document formatting, and does the tool reduce the effort needed to prepare a usable draft or actionable case request?

The first question is an adoption hypothesis raised by the project owner. To test an inversion of adoption priorities, there must be a fair comparison with a general administrative task; feedback on two incentive-related modules alone cannot establish that comparison.

## Rationale

Breadth of applicability and urgency are different dimensions. A generic teaching-document tool may address many teachers, while a particular blocked administrative outcome may create a stronger reason to act today. The corresponding tradeoff is control: a drafting tool can directly improve preparation, while the final allowance process also depends on institutional verification and payment stages.

The public workload literature provides background. A relevant starting reference is Haeri and Afriansyah's 2024 PMM workload study in Aspirasi: https://vs-jurnal.dpr.go.id/index.php/aspirasi/article/view/4615 . The field/workflow descriptions for this project should use the current official references in the source registry. No claim is made that the workload paper evaluated this product or its incentive hypothesis.

## Proposed study

Recruit a small convenience sample of current public SD/SMP teachers and record which genuine tasks they bring. Have participants attempt an existing method and the relevant prototype on comparable examples, varying order where practicable. Include a principal/reviewer or operator to check field acceptance and case routing. Record total effort, edits, omissions and actual next steps.

For recruitment comparisons, use accurately described offers and record the denominator for each channel. Differences between channels, participant status and current deadlines can confound conversion comparisons. A 5–8-person pilot is exploratory, not a national adoption estimate or causal payment study.

## Publication package

Release the original code/docs under MIT, the protocol, synthetic examples, a versioned official-source registry, and consented aggregate findings. Distinguish official requirements, our interpretations, proposed engineering rules, and participant observations. Describe failures and cases the assistant could not resolve.

If language-model assistance is used to draft the article, disclose its role and have human authors verify facts, references and conclusions. Add results only after collecting real evidence.
